Children under one: 75,000 down to 55,000

Those new CSO population figures mentioned above show that the number of children under one in the Republic has fallen from 75,000 in 2010, to 55,000 in 2026.

That is from my column of 24 August 2026. Start there, not with a Church scoreboard. Twenty thousand fewer babies in the first year of life, in sixteen years.

The same column: once fertility rates far well below the replacement level of 2.1, then alarm bells ought to start ringing. Ireland is at about 1.5.

Headlines in those weeks said Catholic baptisms had fallen 30pc in ten years. Tomorrow I will show how to read that 30pc. The under-ones come first, because they are the family story underneath the headline.

David Quinn

The Iona Institute
