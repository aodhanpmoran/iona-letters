Five mornings

  1. The most important thing Most people work to live. Home and the family, not the job, is what comes first.
  2. Mother, wife, children Words missing from a women's strategy, and the aspiration to be at home, for part of life at least.
  3. 75,000 under-ones Children under one in the Republic: 75,000 in 2010, 55,000 in 2026. Then the 30pc baptism headline that tracks those births.
  4. How to read the 30pc Around 60pc of the fall in baptisms is declining births. The rest is rising secularism.
  5. Not a cause for celebration Falling baptisms are a symptom of far fewer children, and hard to celebrate even for the most secular-minded.

The most important thing in their life is home and the family, not their jobs.

David Quinn, Niall Boylan Show, 10 August 2026
