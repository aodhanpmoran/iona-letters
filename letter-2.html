Mother, wife, children

What's missing from the strategy is any mention of mother, any mention of wife, any mention of children. It's so bizarre that something that's central to all our lives is missing from the strategy.

That is what I said on the Niall Boylan Show, episode 854, on 10 August 2026. I also asked: why is marriage missing? Why is family missing? Why do you so de-emphasize the home?

Iona's recap of the appearance, posted 12 August, put the same gap in house wording: the strategy generally has nothing to say about the aspiration of many women to be stay-at-home mothers, for part of their lives at least.

For part of their lives at least. That is not a rule that home is the only path. It is the claim that home is a valid one, and that mother, wife, and children belong in the public argument.
